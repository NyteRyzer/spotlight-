Three studios, one project

Everything shares one timeline, one media bin and one undo history, and it is all autosaved.

Premiere · CapCut · Filmora

🎬 Video Studio

  • Multitrack timeline with insert, overwrite, ripple and razor edits
  • Layered compositing: picture-in-picture, split screen and overlays
  • 16 transitions: dissolves, wipes, slides, iris and zoom
  • Titles and lower thirds in 8 styles, plus SRT captions
  • Speed 0.1×–8×, reverse and freeze frame
  • One-click looks, color controls, green screen and Ken Burns
  • Remove silences, scene detection, beat markers, auto reframe to 9:16
Logic Pro

🎚 Audio Studio

  • Waveform timeline and mixer with volume, pan, mute and solo
  • Denoise, noise gate, de-esser, EQ, compressor and limiter
  • Voice changer, reverb and echo
  • Auto-ducking: the music drops under your voice automatically
  • Record voiceovers while the timeline plays
  • Loudness targets for YouTube (−14 LUFS) and podcasts (−16 LUFS)
Photoshop

🎨 Design Studio

  • Layers of images, text, shapes and brush drawings
  • Blend modes, drop shadow, outer glow and stroke
  • Background removal, crop, fade masks, hue, blur and B&W
  • Thumbnail templates and YouTube safe-zone guides
  • Grab any video frame, and send any design back to the timeline
